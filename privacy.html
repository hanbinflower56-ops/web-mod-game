Privacy Policy

How ModDongGam collects, uses and protects your information when you browse our MOD APK library.

Last updated: January 1, 2026 • Effective date: January 1, 2026

1. Introduction

ModDongGam (“we”, “us”, “our”) operates the website https://modongam.eu.cc (the “Site”), a free catalogue of MOD APK files for Android games. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, how we use it and the choices you have.

By using the Site you agree to this policy. It applies only to our Site, not to third-party websites, advertisers or file hosts you may reach through our links.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information you provide voluntarily

  • Contact details — if you email us (copyright notice, broken-link report or mod request) we receive your email address and whatever you include in that message.
  • Notice details — for DMCA or trademark complaints we may receive your name, company, postal address and supporting documents.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • IP address and approximate region derived from it
  • Browser type and version, operating system, device type and screen size
  • Pages viewed, links clicked, files downloaded and referring URL
  • Date and time of your visit plus a truncated identifier stored in a cookie

2.3 No account required

You do not need to register or log in to browse or download files. We do not knowingly collect sensitive data such as health, biometric or financial information, and we never ask for your game account passwords.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate, maintain and secure the Site
  • To respond to your questions, requests and legal notices
  • To measure traffic, understand which games are popular and improve our content
  • To detect and investigate fraud, abuse, scraping and security incidents
  • To display advertising that funds our free hosting and mirror servers
  • To comply with legal obligations and enforce our rights

4. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR)

For visitors in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland we process personal data on these legal bases:

  • Legitimate interests — running a secure website, preventing abuse and measuring aggregate traffic.
  • Consent — for advertising and analytics cookies that are not strictly necessary. You may withdraw consent at any time.
  • Legal obligation — when we must keep records or answer a lawful request.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device. We use four categories:

  • Strictly necessary — required for the Site to work (security, load balancing, remembering your cookie choice). These cannot be switched off.
  • Functional — remember preferences such as dark theme or search filters.
  • Analytics — show us which pages and games are popular. Data is aggregated and never used to identify you.
  • Advertising — set by our ad partners to display banners and measure how often an ad was shown.

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings or browse in incognito mode. Blocking cookies may break some features, but downloads keep working.

6. Third-Party Advertising and Analytics

ModDongGam is free because it is supported by advertising. We work with third-party networks and analytics providers, which may include PropellerAds, Google AdSense and Google Analytics. They may use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or device identifiers to show relevant adverts, limit how often you see the same advert and measure clicks and conversions.

Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising here:

  • Google Ads Settings — adssettings.google.com
  • Digital Advertising Alliance — aboutads.info
  • European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance — youronlinechoices.eu

Opting out does not remove adverts, it only makes them less relevant. We do not control the data practices of these partners and recommend reading their privacy policies. We may receive aggregate reporting and revenue from ad impressions.

7. Third-Party Links and File Hosts

Download buttons point to external services such as Telegram, MediaFire or a content delivery network. Once you click them you leave ModDongGam and the privacy policy of that provider applies. We are not responsible for the content, security or data practices of external websites.

8. Children’s Privacy

ModDongGam is not directed at children under 13 (under 16 in the EEA) and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it promptly. Many modded games target older audiences, so parental supervision is recommended.

9. Data Retention and Security

  • Server logs (IP address, user agent) — up to 90 days.
  • Analytics reports — aggregated statistics for up to 26 months.
  • Email correspondence — as long as needed to handle your request and defend legal claims.
  • Advertising cookies — for the period defined by each network (typically 13 months).

The Site is served over HTTPS, server access is restricted and we keep our software updated. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security and you use the Site at your own risk.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and similar laws) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal data, to restrict or object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time, and to opt out of the “sale” or “sharing” of personal information — ModDongGam does not sell personal data. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any right, email support@modongam.eu.cc. We may ask for proof of identity and will reply within 30 days (45 days for California residents).

11. International Data Transfers

Our servers and service providers may be located outside your country, including in the United States. Where data is transferred internationally we rely on safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. By using the Site you consent to this transfer.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page always shows the current version and material changes are highlighted here. Continued use of the Site after an update means you accept the revised policy.

13. Contact Us

Questions about privacy can be sent to support@modongam.eu.cc with the subject “Privacy Request”. We aim to reply within 5 business days. Copyright complaints are handled separately — see our DMCA page.
